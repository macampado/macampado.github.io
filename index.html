Hello! I am Micah Acampado, a sophomore at Millersville University. I am majoring in Marketing with a minor in Graphic Communication Technology. After college, I hope to work for a marketing firm doing product marketing, or social media marketing. Originally from Delaware County, PA, I grew up close to the hustle of the city of Philadelphia. I found my love for Marketing and Graphic Communication towards the end of high school.

This website is to showcase a few of the works from AENG 110- Communication and Info Systems. In this class, we have learned how to design, compose, and understand ideas/information. Mainly using graphic and electronic media, we utilized Adobe InDesign. Through the efforts of Dr. Snyder we got hands-on experience with graphic design, desktop publishing, web-page design, digital photography, and digital video.
