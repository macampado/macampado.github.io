Video Project

The Video Production project challenged us to think creatively and make a video to promote the facilities here at Millersville University. Home of Applied Engineering and Safety & Technology, we created a video about Osburn Hall. We toured the various shops and labs the building has to offer. As a group, we had to designate roles to create a storyboard and script. We were able to shoot scenes around Osburn Hall, and edit everything together using iMovie.
